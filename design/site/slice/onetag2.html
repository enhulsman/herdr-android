The design language

Paper and ink, with tag yellow as the brand, lit the way the app icon is. The yellow never becomes a background: it is always the tag itself, an object with an edge and a punched hole, so it stays special and never reads as a warning stripe.

  • One big tag per page

    The tag where the page opens, the app icon where it ends. Everything between is paper and ink.

  • Lit like the app icon

    One soft light from above: the tag runs from light to deep yellow, a rim catches its top edge, and its shadow falls down and to the right. The phone and the icon follow the same light.

  • Yellow is an object, never a ground

    No yellow bands, panels or washes. The hole is always in view, so the shape reads as a tag and not as a bottle or a bell.

  • Ink on yellow, never yellow on paper

    Text on the tag is ink. Yellow never carries text or lines on paper; buttons are ink.

Three steps to your herd.

About ten minutes, once. After that the phone finds your machines on its own. On the Setup page the step you are on is filled.

  1. Install kudde

    Add the GitHub release to Obtainium, or install the APK with adb. You can check the signature first.

  2. Add a machine

    kudde makes a key in your phone's secure hardware. Place it in authorized_keys on the machine and compare the host key.

  3. Turn on alerts

    Point kudde-push at your own ntfy server and send a test alert. It arrives encrypted, end to end.
